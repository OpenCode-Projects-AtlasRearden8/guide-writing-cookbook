# Recipe Card 1: Pick a Topic That Hurts People Today

**Use when:** choosing what your manual is about, or choosing between candidates.

## Find candidates (30 minutes)

1. [ ] List what you helped someone with in the last two weeks.
2. [ ] Collect overheard phrases: "Where do I even start…", "I gave up and paid for…".
3. [ ] For each, name the tax: time, confusion, money, or dignity.

## Score each candidate (15 minutes)

Rate 1–5 per axis; pick the best total.

- [ ] **Hurt** — cost per week in time, money, or dread
- [ ] **Audience** — people reachable who have the pain
- [ ] **Clarity** — can you explain the fix without hand-waving
- [ ] **Freshness** — still true next quarter; stable pain, not news-cycle surface

## The one-sentence pain test (two minutes)

Fill this in honestly:

> This book is for **[person]** who is stuck on **[specific moment]** and wants
> **[specific outcome]** without **[the fear or tax they're avoiding]**.

- [ ] No weasel words ("understanding", "learning about") in any blank.
- [ ] Safety check: education only — no medical, legal, or financial advice.
- [ ] Tie-break toward the topic you could demo to a friend **tonight**.
