# Recipe Card 6: Printables and Tool Ideas

**Use when:** a chapter is drafted and you want it to keep working beyond the book.

## Climb the artifact ladder, pick 2–3 for edition one

1. [ ] **Facts page** — core knowledge, one page, for later reference.
2. [ ] **Checklist** — ordered steps only; a reader's pen can complete it.
3. [ ] **Cheat sheet** — vocabulary, screen names, error messages and meanings.
4. [ ] **Session card** — "do this again six weeks from now" in 6–10 minutes.
5. [ ] **Template** — fill-in-the-blank version of what the reader produces.
6. [ ] **Tool idea** — parked in `ideas.md` until rules are stable and tedium is real.

Rule: every rung must stand alone. A checklist needing the chapter isn't a checklist.

## Printable fitness test

- [ ] One page, prints in black and white, reads at 10.5pt minimum.
- [ ] Works on US Letter **and** A4.
- [ ] No sentences depending on chapter context.
- [ ] Named for the job: "Weekly Dinner Planner," not "Appendix C-7."

## Tool-hunting questions (ask all three)

1. What did the reader compute, count, sort, or copy-paste by hand?
2. What does the chapter ask them to *remember* to check? (A tool beats remembering.)
3. Where did they convert between formats or units? (Small converter.)

## Tool discipline (first edition)

Single file · no accounts · offline · no data collection. If it needs a server:
edition two.

## While drafting

Keep an "artifact candidates" list at the chapter's bottom. Every sentence worth
remembering separately — a warning, a check, a definition — goes on it. Sort it
into the ladder when the chapter is done; delete the list before shipping.
