# Recipe Card 4: Step-by-Step Lesson Rhythms

**Use when:** structuring a lesson, or when a draft "feels long" without being long.

## The beat (repeat every lesson)

1. **Anchor** — 1–3 sentences: where you are → what you'll have. ("In the next ten minutes…")
2. **Setup** — the three things needed on screen. Never more than 5 steps.
3. **Steps** — numbered, in groups of 5 or fewer.
4. **Check** — a sentence where the reader confirms it worked, at least every 5 steps.
5. **Breathe** — one true paragraph: how long the first time takes, what's annoying.
6. **What's next** — one sentence forward. No cliffhangers.

## Rules for tired readers

- [ ] Reader reaches something visibly working inside the first 10 minutes of the book.
- [ ] A named stopping point every ~30 minutes: "Good place to stop."
- [ ] Recovery from the most likely failure is within one paragraph of the step.
- [ ] Prerequisites named *before* the lesson, never at step 4 ("open your config" — which?).

## Pick the lesson genre on purpose

| Type | Opens with | Ends with |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | what you'll have | a check that can't be faked |
| Task | the payoff sentence | the finished artifact |
| Concept | the lie it fixes | one-line restatement |
| Troubleshooter | symptom in reader's words | escalation path |

Troubleshooting = match the symptom, not the internals. "Nothing happens when I click Export," not "export handler errors."

## Rhythm test (on any 3 consecutive lessons)

- [ ] Each opened by telling me where I am?
- [ ] Never more than one lesson between checks?
- [ ] At least one genuine human beat (kindness, honesty, humor)?
- [ ] I could stop after any lesson and return safely?

Three yeses = shipping quality. Two = rebuild the lesson that broke the beat.
